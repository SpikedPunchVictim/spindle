## What the reviewer reported

The review opened by reproducing c569cef's headline figures against a ground-truth
lexer it wrote itself, and confirmed all of them: 150 blind code-context positions
across the whole scan set, all 150 in `crates/spindle-vfs/src/confine/windows.rs`,
0 after the fix; 2 335 blind bytes and 3 353 noise bytes tree-wide, 0 and 0 after.
It then rejected the commit anyway, on the ground that the *verification* behind
those figures had a hole the figures themselves could not show.

The reject finding is the sharpest version of that. The test named
`raw_byte_and_c_string_prefixed_forms_are_masked` asserts the right property in a
form that reads as discriminating. It is not. Deleting the `br`/`cr` branch from
`raw_string_len` leaves every assertion in it true, because the fixture bodies are
re-readable as `b` + a plain string with the same masked result. The suite stayed
at 19 passed / 0 failed against a masker that swallowed a `br`-prefixed instance of
the exact defect the ticket was filed for:

```
in :      let v = relative.starts_with(br"\\?\");
          tracing::warn!(%device_fp, "probe");
neutered: let v = relative.starts_with(br
                                       probe
```

The reviewer also found one real correctness bug the fix had left in place --
`'\x41'` unhandled, masking a real comma -- and one function, `utf8_char_len`,
that no test reached at all. Both are latent in this tree: an exact scan of 83
tracked source files finds zero `'\xNN'` literals and zero non-ASCII char literals.
Fixed anyway, because "this shape does not occur today" is precisely the reasoning
c569cef had just deleted from the old guard's comment, and re-deriving it one
commit later would be the same mistake in a different place.

One correction the review also produced, not recorded as a finding because it is an
omission rather than an error: c569cef reported only the 150 false *greens*. The old
masker also produced 4 false *reds* on the same file -- probes at lines 77, 151, 152
and 158, all inside multi-line strings. Ground truth independently confirms those
four boundaries are non-code. The fix closes both.

## Reading

Five of the six findings are about verification rather than about code, and the one
that is about code (F2) was found by the reviewer's grammar reading, not by the
32 000-input differential fuzz that shipped with the commit. That is the shape worth
keeping from this round.

The commit under review was itself a *correction* commit in spirit: it existed to fix
a guard that had been blind for months. It arrived with an independent ground-truth
lexer, a 7-axis neuter matrix, an exhaustive whole-scan-set probe, and 32 000 fuzz
inputs -- a heavier verification package than almost anything else in this corpus --
and still shipped a test that could not fail and a claim that was not true. The
defence that failed was not "did I check?" but "could my check have seen this?": the
neuter matrix was missing the axis that mattered, and the differential shared its
blind spot with its own reference. Both failures are invisible from inside the
artifact that carries them, and both are cheap to detect from outside it -- one axis
per branch, and one alphabet check against the grammar.

Related to the corpus's headline metric: this is a `rounds/` record where every
finding's `introduced_by_kind` is `original` only in the narrow sense that c569cef
introduced the defective *tests*. The underlying masker bug F2 describes predates it.
The record keeps `original` because the schema asks which commit introduced the thing
the reviewer found wrong, and the thing found wrong was the verification c569cef
shipped.
