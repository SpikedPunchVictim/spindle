## What the commit says

> Sizes are measured, not estimated. A member cap is 466 B of canonical CBOR,
> not the ~330 B §A4 claimed — a 41% understatement in the figure the CONNECT
> budget rests on. A10.10's 32 KiB `max_control_line` survives it: a full 32-cap
> token measures 19,106 B, 42% under the ceiling. So the conclusion was right on
> a number that was wrong, and both are now recorded.
>
> That measurement is what forced the QR ceiling into the design. At ~530 B per
> entry a version-40 QR carries 4 hosts at EC level M and 5 at L, against the
> 32-host presentation cap — an order of magnitude short.

## Reading

DESIGN.md §A4 had pinned an estimated cap size (~330 B) that a downstream budget calculation (the 32 KiB `max_control_line` ceiling) depended on. When the implementer needed the real bundle entry size for the new QR-ceiling design work, measuring the actual canonical-CBOR encoding revealed the estimate was 41% low. The budget conclusion happened to survive the correction (19,106 B is still comfortably under 32 KiB), but the commit is explicit that this was luck on a wrong number, not proof the number was ever validated — exactly the "pinned figures need their basis validated" failure mode this repo's own working notes call out. This is the implementer's own later measurement catching an unmeasured figure that had been sitting in the shipped design doc, not a claim about test coverage or code correctness.
