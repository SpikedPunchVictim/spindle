## Reading

CI's formatting gate had been red on every push for at least two days across five crates/spike areas, and nobody noticed or acted on it — the local pre-commit routine checked everything CI checks except the one gate that runs first. This is a missing-guard finding in the "enforced in one place, not its sibling" sense: CI enforced `fmt --check`, the local workflow didn't mirror it, so the gap was invisible until someone actually looked at the CI history. The fix closes the gap structurally (local gates now run every CI step) rather than just fixing the accumulated diff, which is the right shape for this class of defect.
