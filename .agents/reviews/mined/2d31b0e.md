## Reading

This is a striking case: the very commit written to close a false-green defect (3f72388, itself catching an earlier false-green) shipped a false green of its own, and the review demonstrated it by probing the real server rather than assuming its behavior — settling an empirical question (does nats-server honor the first or last occurrence of a duplicate config key?) that the original test writer had apparently assumed rather than measured. The reproduction is exact: appending a second, later `max_control_line: 8192` line left the new "config agreement" test green while the deployed server would silently drop every real 32-cap CONNECT token, which is precisely the failure mode td-db47f9 exists to prevent. Two secondary doc-comment overclaims from the same commit were also disproven by direct experiment (deleting a guard and re-running the test; noting the duplicate-key hole falsifies the stated "cannot drift apart" guarantee), continuing the pattern from the td-331c11 lineage of claims about test coverage needing to be tested themselves.
