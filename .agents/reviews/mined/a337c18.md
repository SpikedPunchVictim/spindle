## Reading

An independent adversarial review of e8480af — the commit that had just fixed a credential leak and two other data-leak defects — found two more problems of exactly the shape e8480af itself had warned about. The first is almost a category error preserved for its honesty: e8480af correctly found and fixed the credential-leak bug, but cited the wrong line/macro for where it surfaced, because the audit methodology that found the OTHER leaks (grepping `tracing::` macros) was structurally blind to this leak's actual sink (a plain `eprintln!`), so the citation was guessed rather than traced. The second is more serious: an entire error variant (`SignalingError::Ice`, reachable from attacker-addressable UDP before any envelope verification) had never been examined by the redaction wrapper's enumeration at all, falling through a catch-all `safe` arm — precisely the "half-applied check" pattern e8480af's own message had named as the danger to watch for one commit earlier. Both findings are recorded with a receipt (an explicit sweep of every `eprintln!`/`println!` site, and a trace of what bytes are actually reachable through the current locked dependency versions) rather than asserted from memory.
