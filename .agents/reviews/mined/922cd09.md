## Reading

Two of the four "Confirmed" findings in the implementer's own 2026-09-02
bug-hunt report were themselves false, and the commit names one shared root
cause: a repo-only grep returning zero matches was read as proof the behavior
was absent, when the behavior in both cases (a busy timeout, foreign-key
enforcement) was actually supplied by the vendored SQLite/rusqlite
dependency rather than by anything a repo grep could see — and in the
foreign-key case, the repo's own schema.rs comment already contradicted the
finding and was never read. This is a clean instance of `false-absence-claim`
twice over, plus a third, smaller correction to a refutation-log entry whose
conclusion was right but whose stated mechanism (an immediate lock error
rather than a wait-then-zero-rows outcome) was wrong — `wrong-mechanism`. All
three retractions come from the implementer's own later re-verification of
their own filing, not an outside reviewer.
