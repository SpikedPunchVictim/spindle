## Reading

The previous commit (6bf12c76) had graduated the S2 spike's signaling flow into production code, marked the slice done, and shipped it behind a fully green 483-test unit suite — but that suite was entirely in-process and never actually drove a NATS server, an ICE punch, or a QUIC handshake. When this commit finally ran the real flow live, it found the code could not connect at all, for two independent and each individually fatal reasons: a host's two distinct fingerprints (subject-scoping vs. envelope identity) had been collapsed into one field during graduation, and the call to actually start ICE connectivity checks had simply been dropped somewhere between the spike and the graduated crate. Both defects are concrete, reproducible, and directly attributable to the earlier commit; the author explicitly draws the parallel to 867b806's false-green pattern — tests that are green because they never exercise the real path, not because the path works.
