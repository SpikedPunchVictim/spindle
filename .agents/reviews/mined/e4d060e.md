## What the commit says

> A worktree under .claude/worktrees was nearly committed as an embedded
> gitlink by a broad git add during the slice-4 merge (caught and amended
> out). Ignore the directory so it cannot happen again.

## Reading

A broad `git add` during the Stage 6 slice 4 merge nearly captured a session-local agent worktree as an embedded gitlink in the superproject's history — the kind of mistake that, if pushed, corrupts checkouts for everyone else who clones the repo. It was caught and removed via amend before it became permanent, which is why the fix here is preventive (a `.gitignore` entry) rather than a revert of shipped history. There's no missing-guard-in-a-sibling pattern here — this is a single guard (an ignore rule) that simply didn't exist yet for a category of file the repo hadn't produced before (agent worktrees), so `missing-guard` is a loose fit but the closest available slug; nothing in the vocabulary specifically covers "near-miss caught before push."
