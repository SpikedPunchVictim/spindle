## What the commit says

> e69d6a7 claimed to close the S18 minting TOCTOU by reading membership and
> `cap_epoch` under one `Mutex<Store>`. An independent adversarial review
> proved that claim false, and reproduced the defect with this repo's own
> types. This is the actual fix.
>
> Why the mutex was never enough. `Store` wraps a single rusqlite
> `Connection`, and neither read opened a transaction... And
> `spindle-hostd` opens three independent connections to the same file. A
> `revoke_*_and_bump_epoch` committing on one of those lands cleanly between
> the two reads and never touches the mutex, yielding the pre-revoke-member +
> post-bump-epoch pair -- a validly-signed cap for a revoked subject that
> looks freshly-issued to any consumer checking `cap.cap_epoch` against the
> host's current epoch.
>
> The comment e69d6a7 put inside that method said `spindle-hostd` "holds
> multiple independent connections to the same database file". The reason
> the approach could not work was written into the approach.
>
> Known gap, stated plainly rather than papered over: commenting out the
> `unchecked_transaction` leaves the entire suite green (independently
> neuter-verified, 292 tests across spindle-vfs and spindle-host-core still
> pass).

## Reading

e69d6a7 shipped a fix for a genuine security TOCTOU — minting a member capability from two non-atomic reads could hand out a validly-signed cap for a subject that had been revoked in the gap between reads — but the fix itself was wrong: an in-process `Mutex<Store>` cannot provide atomicity across independent SQLite connections to the same file, and `spindle-hostd` opens exactly three such connections. The commit even notes the irony that e69d6a7's own comment documented the multi-connection fact that doomed its own mechanism. An independent adversarial review reproduced the un-fixed race with the repo's real types and forced the correct fix down into `Store` as a genuine `unchecked_transaction`. The commit is also honest that the new fix ships with a known verification gap of its own (the entire suite stays green with the transaction commented out) — a gap closed two commits later in 21723f7.
